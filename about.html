Who We Are

Our Story

AL FAKHAMA METAL TRADING L.L.C is a Dubai-based scrap metal trading company focused on the sourcing, trading and responsible handling of recyclable metal materials. We work with automotive and spare-parts companies, workshops, factories, warehouses, construction companies and commercial businesses, providing professional solutions for surplus and recyclable materials.

Our approach is simple — reliable service, transparent communication, competitive market-based pricing and long-term business relationships. We believe scrap metal is not simply waste. It is a valuable resource that can be recovered, traded and given a new purpose.
